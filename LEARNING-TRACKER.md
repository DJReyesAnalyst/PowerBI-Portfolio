# 🚨 When I Fall Behind

### Missed 1–7 days

Continue from the last unchecked task.

### Missed 1–2 weeks

Spend one review session on the previous phase, then continue.

### Missed more than 2 weeks

Do NOT restart from Week 1.

Use this reset:

1. Read the phase README.
2. Review your last project.
3. Complete one small hands-on exercise.
4. Update this tracker.
5. Continue from the next unchecked item.

### Feeling overwhelmed

Use the **Minimum Viable Study Session**:

> 25 minutes learning + 35 minutes hands-on + 5 minutes documentation.

Even one completed exercise keeps the chain alive.
